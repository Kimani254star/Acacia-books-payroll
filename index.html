Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

What does a pay run do?

It takes your employee records, allowances, deductions and statutory taxes and works out each person's pay for the period.

Can employees get payslips?

Yes. A payslip is generated for each employee in a pay run.

Can I set up allowances and deductions?

Yes. Allowances, deductions and statutory taxes are set up in Configuration and applied in your pay runs.

Is my data kept per company?

Yes. Each company has its own separate data, and everyone in that company sees the same records.
